Data Management & Backup

Create Central backups, queue sensor SQLite backups, or reset selected data. Destructive actions require typing RESET. Central data resets preserve the authentication/control plane (users, sessions, built-in roles, protected administrator and enrolled sensors/sites). Full reset also preserves core operator/reference policy; managed rule sets and imported data are resettable. Resets that have a sensor-side equivalent are also queued to enrolled sensors so deleted data is not immediately re-uploaded.
